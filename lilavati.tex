To those on Earth whom Līlāvatī keeps in her embrace,\\[0.3em]
Of noble birth, her form with virtues pure adorned,\\[0.3em]
Who hearken to her speech, instinct with grace,\\[0.3em]
Increased be their joy, for evermore.

\upshape
\vspace{1.5em}
\hfill Bhāskarācārya, from \textit{Līlāvatī}

% Version B

To those on Earth whom Līlāvatī claspeth in her embrace
Of noble birth, with virtues most pure her form adorned
And her speech instinct with grace
The treasure of their happiness ever waxeth more.
